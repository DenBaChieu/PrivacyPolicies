Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 17, 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Sinamics Fault Finder handles user data. Our app is designed to search fault codes using pre-existing data within the app. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

We do not collect any personal data, usage data, or device information. The app may store user preferences locally on the device to enhance the user experience, but this information never leaves the device or is shared externally.

3. How We Use Information

Since we do not collect any data, there is no information to process or use. Any locally stored preferences remain on the user's device and are not transmitted.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app does not integrate with or rely on third-party services that collect user data.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Users can review the latest version of this Privacy Policy within the app or on our website, if applicable.

6. Legal Compliance

This Privacy Policy complies with applicable data protection laws, including GDPR and CCPA, as our app does not process or collect personal information.
