Privacy Policy for Auto Calendar

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 13, 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Auto Calendar handle your information when you use our service to convert text into calendar events using Gemini AI and add them to your Google Calendar.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our website, we process:

  • User-provided text - The text you enter to generate calendar events.
  • Google Calendar data - Event details necessary to create events in your calendar.
  • OAuth information - Your Google account credentials (access token and refresh token) for authentication.

3. How We Use Information

We use your information only to:

  • Interpret your text using the Gemini AI API to create event details.
  • Add those events to your Google Calendar via the Google Calendar API.
We do not:
  • Sell your information.
  • Use your data for advertising purposes.
  • Store your event details after they are added to your calendar.

4. Data Processing and Storage

  • All processing happens in real-time.
  • We temporarily store your Google OAuth access token and refresh token for up to 15 minutes to complete the event creation process.
  • These credentials are automatically deleted from our system after this period.
  • We do not store your calendar event content beyond the time needed for processing.

5. Sharing of Information

Your data is shared only with:

  • Google APIs - To create events in your Google Calendar.
  • Gemini AI API - To process and interpret your text into event details.
We do not share your information with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

6. User Control

You can revoke our access to your Google account at any time through your Google Account Permissions.

7. Security

We use HTTPS and industry-standard encryption to protect data in transit. Temporary credentials are stored securely and deleted automatically after processing.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new "Last Updated".
